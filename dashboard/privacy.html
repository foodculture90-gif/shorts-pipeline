Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mission Control is a private tool used only by its owner to create short videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What we access

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, Mission Control asks Google for permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel and video information such as titles and view counts (youtube.readonly).

How it is used

This access is used only to upload videos the owner has approved and to show their view counts in the owner's dashboard. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

The permission token is stored encrypted-at-rest in the owner's private database and is only readable by the owner's own cloud worker. Video files are deleted after they are published.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Mission Control's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
